Privacy Policy — Family Schedule

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Family Schedule is a private home dashboard used only by its owner's family.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the temperature and humidity from the owner's Nest thermostat through Google's Smart Device Management API.

How the data is used

The readings are shown on a screen in the owner's home. They are not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Sign-in details stay on the owner's own computer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
